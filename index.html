What is SkyBlock?

SkyBlock is a long-running survival game mode on Hypixel. You keep one character profile across seasons, so every hour you play adds to something permanent.

You begin with almost nothing. Early on you break cobblestone and chop wood. Later you run minions that work while you're away, clear dungeons with a party, and sell loot on the Auction House and Bazaar.

There's no single way to win. Some players chase the strongest weapon, some farm crops for coins, and some just decorate their island.

What you can do

  • Private island

    Your own space to build on, expand and invite friends to.

  • Minions

    Place them on your island and they collect resources over time.

  • Skills and collections

    Mining, farming, combat and more. Each level unlocks recipes and perks.

  • Dungeons

    Team up and fight through floors of mobs and bosses for gear.

  • Bazaar and Auction House

    Trade coins for items with other players across the server.

  • Pets

    Level up companions that boost your stats while you play.

Ranks

Ranks are optional. They add a coloured name tag and some convenience perks, and you can enjoy SkyBlock without one.

  • [VIP]

    The entry rank. A green name tag and basic perks.

  • [VIP+]

    VIP with a gold plus and a few extra perks.

  • [MVP]

    An aqua name tag and a bigger perk set.

  • [MVP+]

    MVP with a plus colour you can change.

  • [MVP++]

    The monthly subscription rank with a gold name tag.

Perks and prices change. Check the official Hypixel Store for the current list.

How to join

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition and go to Multiplayer.
  2. Add the server with the address mc.hypixel.net.
  3. Join, then type /play skyblock in chat or click right with compass and select "Skyblock"
  4. Talk to the NPCs on your island and follow the first quests.

Rules

Short version: play fair and be decent. Hypixel enforces its own rules, so read them before you join.

  • No cheating

    No hacked clients, macros that give an edge, or exploiting bugs.

  • Be respectful

    No harassment, hate speech or threats in chat or on islands.

  • No scamming

    Do not trick players out of coins or items in trades.

  • No spam or ads

    Keep chat clean and do not advertise other servers.

  • Protect your account

    Never share your password or log in through links from strangers.

  • Follow staff

    If a moderator asks you to stop something, stop.

FAQ

Is SkyBlock free?

Yes. You can play for free. Ranks and cosmetics are optional.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Hypixel is a Java Edition server. Check the official site for current platform support.

What should I do first?

Talk to the NPCs on your island, finish the starter quests, then set up your first minions.

Do I lose progress between seasons?

No. Your profile and items stay with you.
